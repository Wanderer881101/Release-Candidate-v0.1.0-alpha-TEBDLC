# TEBDLC — Territorial Rights Policy v0.2

Status: **ACTIVE PROJECT POLICY FOR PUBLIC v0.1.0-alpha RELEASE CANDIDATE**

This policy supersedes the earlier publication-boundary portions of `TERRITORIAL_DISTRIBUTION_POLICY.md` for the public `v0.1.0-alpha` Release Candidate.

## 1. Public availability

The public Release Candidate is intentionally globally readable and downloadable. Territorial classification does not claim to prevent technical access to bytes that are already published in the public GitHub repository.

Public availability and project-granted rights are distinct.

## 2. General public-release rights

Rights to inspect, build, execute, test, benchmark, debug and privately evaluate the public `v0.1.0-alpha` Release Candidate are defined by `LICENSE-SOURCE-AVAILABLE-v0.1.1.md`.

Territorial classification must not be used to contradict those public-release permissions.

## 3. Territorial states retained for governed project activities

The historical classifications `PRIVILEGED`, `NEUTRAL` and `RESTRICTED` remain available as project-governance metadata for activities that are not inherent in merely reading or using the public Release Candidate, including where applicable:

- participation in the formal TEBDLC falsification registry/regime;
- access to future private artifacts not included in the public Release Candidate;
- controlled services or private delivery mechanisms introduced by a later explicitly versioned release;
- other project-governed privileges explicitly bound to a territorial policy version.

A later policy must state exactly which right is territorialized. No territorial restriction is inferred merely from public source visibility.

## 4. Historical v0.1 classifications

The classifications and annexes recorded under v0.1 remain preserved as historical governance/provenance. They are not deleted by this policy.

Where `TERRITORIAL_DISTRIBUTION_POLICY.md` states that the complete usable `v0.1.0-alpha` product must remain outside the public repository or that the public repository is not its distribution endpoint, that publication-boundary statement is superseded by:

- `ACTIVE_RELEASE_MODEL_v0.1.0-alpha.md`;
- `LICENSE-SOURCE-AVAILABLE-v0.1.1.md`;
- this policy.

## 5. Formal falsification participation

Formal TEBDLC falsification participation may remain subject to project eligibility rules and provenance requirements. Ordinary independent testing, criticism, benchmarking or debugging of the public Release Candidate is not converted into formal-registry participation merely because it challenges a TEBDLC claim.

## 6. Future controlled distribution

The historical controlled-distribution subsystem under `distribution/` may be retained and evolved for future private artifacts or services. If used in the future, it must bind to an explicitly identified non-public artifact or service and a current policy version. It does not retroactively make this public Release Candidate private.

## 7. Mandatory law

This is a project governance policy, not legal advice or a representation of governmental or judicial certification. Mandatory applicable law prevails where required.

## 8. Non-retroactivity and provenance

Historical distribution events, hashes, acceptance records, validation results and policy versions remain historical facts. This policy changes the active public-release architecture prospectively and does not rewrite those records.
